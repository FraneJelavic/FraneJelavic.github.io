Hi, I’m Frane Jelavić, a Principal Engineer at Infobip.

I design and operate database-heavy distributed systems, focusing on high availability, change data capture, reliability, and performance. I work with PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Kafka, Debezium, Go, and Java.

Much of my work starts when production systems stop behaving as expected: replication failures, slow queries, lagging data pipelines, unreliable failover, or database migrations that must remain online. I trace those problems through the database, application, and infrastructure to understand the mechanism.

This site is where I document what I learn and how I reason about operational decisions. Some pieces remain working notes. Others are published by ShiftMag and the Infobip Developers Hub.
