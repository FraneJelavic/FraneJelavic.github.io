This site is hosted by [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), which may process technical information needed to serve the site.

In production, this site uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy) to understand aggregate visits. The integration does not set cookies or send custom identifying events. The analytics script is not loaded during local development.
